# SDR & Amateur Radio Resources

## Knowledge

- [PySDR: A Guide to SDR and DSP using Python by Dr. Marc Lichtman](https://pysdr.org/)
  Visual, hands-on, modern guide to I/Q sampling, digital modulation, filters, and synchronization. Use for: intuitive I/Q concepts, matched filtering, and frequency/phase representations.
- [Software Defined Radio with HackRF by Michael Ossmann (Great Scott Gadgets)](https://greatscottgadgets.com/sdr/)
  The definitive foundational video course on SDR. Use for: sample rate intuition, aliasing, complex baseband representation, and SDR hardware fundamentals.
- [The ARRL Handbook for Radio Communications](https://www.arrl.org/arrl-handbook-reference)
  The premier reference for amateur radio theory, RF front-ends, transmission lines, and digital communication modes.
- [GNU Radio Guided Tutorials & Architecture Manual](https://wiki.gnuradio.org/index.php/Tutorials)
  The standard SDR DSP framework. Use for: comparing streaming flowgraph architectures, buffer management, and polyphase channelizers.
- [Mode-S and ADS-B The 1090MHz Riddle by Junzi Sun (The Book of ADS-B)](https://mode-s.org/decode/)
  Primary source for Mode-S framing, PPM bit slicing, and preamble physics. Use as reference implementation data.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/amateurradio](https://reddit.com/r/amateurradio)
  Active community of licensed amateur radio operators discussing rigs, antennas, and digital modes.
- [r/RTLSDR](https://reddit.com/r/RTLSDR)
  SDR enthusiasts hacking on low-cost dongles, antennas, and demodulators across VHF/UHF/satellite bands.
- [Amateur Radio Stack Exchange](https://ham.stackexchange.com/)
  High-signal Q&A community for technical radio engineering, antenna theory, and operating practices.
