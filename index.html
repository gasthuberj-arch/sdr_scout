SDR Systems Architecture & DSP Portal

An engineering-grade learning workspace dissecting high-throughput software-defined radio systems, digital signal processing (I/Q baseband, filtering, correlation), and modern concurrent architectures (Elixir/OTP + Zig NIFs vs. GNU Radio vs. Pure C/Rust).

Available Lessons

  • Lesson 0001: SDR Pipeline Architectures & Trade-offs
    Why combine Elixir and Zig? Zero-copy binary passing, GenStage demand backpressure, the 1ms NIF trap, and suitability for HAM radio networks.
  • Lesson 0002: Complex Baseband (I/Q) & SIMD Vector Processing
    Euler's formula, the negative frequency ambiguity, quadrature mixing, complex Nyquist bandwidth ($B = f_s$), AoS vs. SoA layouts, L2 cache fit, and @Vector(16, f32) SIMD magnitude.
  • Lesson 0003: Carrier Frequency Offsets, Non-Coherent Detection, and Chunk Boundaries
    Mathematical derivation of phase rotation ($\Delta\phi = 2\pi\Delta f T$), why non-coherent magnitude $|z|$ bypasses Costas loops, the 0.73% chunk boundary loss theorem, and why persistent NIF tail-buffers beat Linux vm_circbuf double-mmap on the BEAM.
  • Lesson 0004: Matched Filters, Preamble Correlation, and GF(2) Syndromes
    Cauchy-Schwarz derivation of the matched filter's optimal $2E_s/N_0$ SNR bound, differential correlation vs. SIMD vector division penalties, and $O(1)$ single-bit error recovery via linear polynomial syndromes over $\text{GF}(2)$.
  • Lesson 0005: Demystifying DSP — Vector Projections, APRS, and FT8
    Bridging DSP to linear algebra: why every filter is just a vector dot product ($\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y} = \|\mathbf{x}\|\|\mathbf{y}\|\cos\theta$), building an APRS 1200 baud AFSK demodulator in 10 lines, and how FT8 pulls signals from -24 dB noise via coherent processing gain ($N^2 / N$).
  • Lesson 0006: Phase Invariance, I/Q Quadrature Projections, and the Rayleigh Limit
    Why a single cosine template drops out completely at $90^\circ$ phase shift, achieving phase invariance via dual I/Q projections ($I^2 + Q^2$), the Rayleigh tone resolution bound ($\Delta f \cdot \Delta t \ge 1$), and the DFT as a bank of orthogonal dot products.
  • Lesson 0007: Symbol Timing Recovery, the Eye Diagram, and the Gardner Detector
    Why independent transmitter/receiver crystals drift into transition edges, reading the Eye Diagram, and the mathematical elegance of the Gardner Timing Error Detector ($e[k] = x[k-1/2](x[k] - x[k-1])$) paired with fractional interpolators.
  • Lesson 0008: Polyphase Filterbanks — Channelizing 32 Radios on One CPU Core
    The 10-billion MACs/sec naive channelizer trap, the Noble Identity (decimating before filtering), decomposing prototype filters into parallel polyphase branches, and using an IFFT for a 440x computational speedup.
  • Lesson 0009: From Radio Waves to Cloud Security — SDR & DSP in DevSecOps
    Bridging DSP to your day job: GPS/PTP spoofing attacks on cloud data center consensus (Spanner/CockroachDB), detecting C2 malware beaconing in proxy logs via FFT and autocorrelation, and applying zero-copy backpressure to eBPF/SIEM firehoses.
  • Lesson 0010: Live Codebase Tour — Inside SdrScout's Zigler SIMD & GenStage
    Walking the production implementation: persistent DspContext resource memory, 16-lane SIMD magnitude, in-place $O(1)$ syndrome recovery, GenStage bounded demand contracts, and 27.5 MSPS (13.8x real-time) benchmarks.
  • Lesson 0011: Clustering SDRs — Multilateration (TDOA) and Distributed BEAM Nodes
    Connecting two Portenta X8s with RTL-SDRs: the physics of hyperbolic TDOA ($300\text{ m}/\mu\text{s}$), $O(N \log N)$ frequency-domain cross-correlation ($R_{xy} = \mathcal{F}^{-1}\{X \cdot Y^*\}$), native distributed Erlang clustering, and overcoming the nanosecond clock sync barrier via GPS PPS or opportunistic reference towers.
  • Lesson 0012: Deep TDOA — GCC-PHAT, Sub-Sample Fitting, & Hyperbolic Solvers
    Going deep into real SIGINT/TDOA: why standard correlation smears in multipath, phase whitening via GCC-PHAT ($R = \mathcal{F}^{-1}\{(XY^*)/|XY^*|\}$) to produce a Dirac delta, 3-point parabolic peak interpolation cutting 150m coarseness to ~5m, and Bancroft's spacetime Minkowski solver.
