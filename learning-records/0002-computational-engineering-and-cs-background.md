# Computational Engineering & CS Background Established

The user holds a Computational Engineering BSc and Computer Science MSc, with strong foundations in C, Rust, cloud, security, and systems architecture. They specifically asked for deeper exposure to DSP and rigorous trade-off analysis across SDR architectures.

## Implications
- Calibrate teaching to graduate-level computer science and computational mathematics: we can use formal mathematical notation (Fourier analysis, complex baseband $I(t) + jQ(t)$, Euler's formula, convolution, autocorrelation) alongside systems-level details (SIMD AVX-512/NEON intrinsics, MMU circular buffer aliasing, cache line utilization, soft vs. hard realtime).
- Treat HAM radio modes (APRS, FT8, WSPR, AX.25, DMR) as concrete case studies for different DSP challenges: audio-rate MFSK vs. RF-rate baseband, coherent vs. non-coherent detection, phase lock loops (PLL), and Costas loops.
